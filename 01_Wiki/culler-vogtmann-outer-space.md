# Culler–Vogtmann Outer Space

A concept in geometric group theory — unrelated to physical [[outer-space]] despite sharing the name. Introduced by Marc Culler and Karen Vogtmann in a 1986 paper.

## Definition

For a free group *Fₙ*, the Culler–Vogtmann Outer Space (denoted *Xₙ* or *CVₙ*) is a topological space consisting of "marked metric graph structures" of volume 1 on *Fₙ*. It carries a natural action of Out(*Fₙ*) — the outer automorphism group of *Fₙ*.

Equivalently: the set of *Fₙ*-equivariant isometry types of minimal free discrete isometric actions of *Fₙ* on **R**-trees *T* such that the quotient metric graph *T*/*Fₙ* has volume 1.

## Role in Mathematics

Serves as the free group analog of **Teichmüller space** (which plays the same role for hyperbolic surfaces). Used to study:

- Homology and cohomology groups of Out(*Fₙ*)
- Algebraic, geometric, and dynamical properties of Out(*Fₙ*) and its subgroups
- Properties of individual outer automorphisms of *Fₙ*

## Disambiguation

The name collision with physical outer space is purely coincidental — the mathematical object is named because it is the natural space on which *outer* automorphisms act, not for any astronomical reason.
