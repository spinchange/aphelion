# Crossing: Venus / Aphrodite

**The most beautiful object in the sky — mapped to the goddess of beauty.**

But the crossing is deeper than a colour match. Venus's behaviour in the sky — its disappearance and reappearance, its dual role as morning and evening star, its descent below the horizon and return — was encoded in mythology **before** Greek civilisation, in the Sumerian myths of Inanna.

## The Astronomical Character

Venus is the brightest object in the sky after Sun and Moon — sometimes bright enough to cast shadows. It is always near the Sun (max ~47° elongation), appearing only at dawn or dusk. It *vanishes* from evening skies as it passes inferior conjunction, then *reappears* as a morning star. This cycle takes ~584 days.

Additionally: Venus rotates **retrograde** — backwards. From Venus, the Sun rises in the west. And its day is longer than its year. It is, physically, the most alien of the inner planets despite being Earth's closest neighbour.

## The Hesperus/Phosphorus Discovery

The Greeks believed the morning and evening appearances of Venus were two different stars — **Phosphorus** (dawn-bringer) and **Hesperus** (western one). The discovery that they were one object is credited to Pythagoras or Parmenides in the 6th–5th century BC.

But **Babylonians already knew** this — the *Venus Tablet of Ammisaduqa* (possibly 2nd millennium BC) records Venus's appearances as a unified cycle. The Sumerians tracking Inanna's movements understood the single object even earlier.

## The Inanna Encoding

The deepest version of this crossing predates Aphrodite by millennia. **Inanna's Descent to the Underworld** describes the goddess descending through seven gates, being killed, then resurrected and returning. Theology professor Jeffrey Cooley has argued this myth directly parallels Venus's disappearance in the west (inferior conjunction) and reappearance in the east — a mythological encoding of planetary motion.

Inanna → Ishtar (Babylonian) → Astarte (Phoenician) → Aphrodite (Greek). Each is associated with the same planet. Each governs love, beauty, and war. The chain of transmission carries the astronomical knowledge embedded in the myth across 3,000 years of civilisations.

## Crossing Type

`observed` — Venus's extraordinary brightness and behaviour is directly, obviously visible. The beauty association is immediate. The morning/evening star unification required only careful observation. The Inanna myth suggests the encoding may be deliberate, reaching back beyond Greece to Sumer.
