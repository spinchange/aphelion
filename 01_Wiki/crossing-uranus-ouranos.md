# Crossing: Uranus / Ouranos

**The primordial sky — mapped to the first planet beyond the known cosmos.**

This crossing has two dimensions: a deliberate naming that followed mythological logic, and a resonance between the myth's character and the planet's astronomical position that the namers may or may not have consciously recognised.

## The Deliberate Layer

When William Herschel discovered the planet in 1781, he named it **Georgium Sidus** after King George III. The astronomical community refused this political choice. The German astronomer Johann Bode proposed **Uranus** — following what he recognised as the pattern of planetary mythology:

The sequence of mythological succession: **Ouranos → Cronus → Zeus** (each overthrows his father)

The sequence of planets outward from the Sun: **Jupiter (Zeus) → Saturn (Cronus) → Uranus (Ouranos)**

The naming logic: if Jupiter overthrew Saturn, and Saturn overthrew Uranus, then the planet beyond Saturn should be named for Ouranos — Saturn's father, who came before. The mythological family tree **maps to the orbital hierarchy** going outward.

This is `observed` — the namers consciously followed this logic. The crossing is deliberate in its naming, not accidental.

## The Resonance Layer

What makes this crossing richer is what Ouranos *is* in mythology and what Uranus *is* in the Solar System:

**Ouranos** is the primordial, formless, unbounded sky — not a being with character or cult, but the sky itself. He precedes order, precedes the Titan age, precedes Olympian governance. He is what existed before structure. After his castration he "recedes into the background" and becomes the passive, empty sky.

**Uranus the planet** is the first body beyond the five naked-eye planets that organised ancient astronomy and mythology. For all of human history up to 1781, the planetary cosmos consisted of Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn — the seven classical bodies (with Sun and Moon) that defined the week, the zodiac, and the mythology. **Uranus lies beyond this known boundary** — the first thing in a region that was, for all prior humanity, simply sky.

In other words: Ouranos was what existed *before* the ordered cosmos; Uranus the planet is what lies *beyond* the ordered cosmos, in the realm that was previously indistinguishable from sky.

## The Aphrodite Chain

The mythological castration also creates a chain that now traverses the planetary system:

**Ouranos (Uranus)** castrated by Cronus → genitals into the sea → foam → **Aphrodite (Venus)**

The planet Uranus → the planet Saturn → the planet Venus: the mythological causal chain now connects the outermost planets to the brightest inner one, spanning the Solar System's width in a single narrative.

## Crossing Type

`observed` — the naming was deliberate, following mythological logic. The deeper resonance (boundless-primordial-sky = beyond-the-known-cosmos) was probably not the primary motivation but is real.

Unlike `accidental` crossings (Mercury/Hermes), this is a case where humans *chose* to impose a mythological correspondence, and the correspondence turned out to be apt in ways they may not have fully intended.
