## The Crown Jewels

### 2027 August 2 — The Eclipse of a Lifetime
The centerline passes over **Luxor, Egypt** — the ancient astronomical and archaeological capital of the ancient world — during 6 minutes 23 seconds of totality. This is not merely the longest eclipse of the 21st century; it is the longest until **2150**. The path runs west to east across North Africa and the Arabian Peninsula in the height of summer. The site has been used for astronomical observation since at least 3000 BCE.

**Viewing regions**: Morocco (Agadir, Marrakech region), Algeria, Libya, Egypt (Luxor, Aswan, Hurghada area), Saudi Arabia (Mecca/Medina region), Yemen, Somalia.

### 2045 August 12 — The American Eclipse
Sweeps across the western and southern United States: northern California through Nevada, Utah, Colorado, Kansas, Oklahoma, Arkansas, Louisiana, and Florida. Duration up to 6 minutes 6 seconds — among the longest ever for US soil. The Caribbean and northern South America also see totality.

### 2026 August 12 — Coming in 98 Days
The most immediate opportunity. Centerline crosses mainland Spain — including Valencia and the Balearic Islands — making it highly accessible from Europe. Duration is modest (~2m 18s) but this is a straightforward trip for anyone in Western Europe.

## Planning Notes

- **Eye safety**: Never look at the partial phases without certified eclipse glasses (ISO 12312-2). Totality only is safe for naked-eye viewing.
- **Path width matters**: The path of totality is typically only 100–200 km wide. Positioning within the centerline maximises duration.
- **Weather**: Clear skies are everything. Egypt (2027) and Australia (2028) offer excellent eclipse-weather odds. Spain (2026) is reliable in August.
- **NASA eclipse maps**: https://eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov — interactive path maps for every eclipse

## Connection to Aphelion

Total solar eclipses are moments when the [[solar-corona]] becomes visible to the naked eye — the only time most people ever see it. The [[coronal-mass-ejection|CME]] history, the [[space-weather]] the Sun drives, the corona heating mystery that [[parker-solar-probe]] is investigating — all of this becomes directly visible during those few minutes. Eclipses are also the historical mechanism by which the [[crossing-mercury-hermes|Mercury proximity insight]] and [[crossing-orion-scorpius|Orion/Scorpius sky mechanics]] were first studied: the Babylonians tracked astronomical cycles precisely enough to predict eclipses centuries in advance (see [[babylonian-astronomy]]).
