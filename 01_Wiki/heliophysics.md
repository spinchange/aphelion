# Heliophysics

The scientific study of the [[heliosphere]] and the Sun's influence on the Solar System — encompassing space weather, space climate, and the interactions between solar output and planetary environments. Closely linked to the study of [[geospace]], since solar activity drives geomagnetic storms, auroras, and radiation belt dynamics.

Heliophysics includes the study of:

- The solar wind and its propagation through the [[interplanetary-medium]]
- The structure and boundaries of the heliosphere (termination shock, heliosheath, [[heliopause]])
- Space weather events (coronal mass ejections, solar flares) and their effects on Earth and spacecraft
- The [[van-allen-radiation-belts]] and magnetospheric dynamics
- Interactions between the heliosphere and the [[interstellar-medium]]

NASA's heliophysics missions have included the Voyager program, IBEX (Interstellar Boundary Explorer), the Van Allen Probes, and the Interstellar Mapping and Acceleration Probe (IMAP, launched 2025).
