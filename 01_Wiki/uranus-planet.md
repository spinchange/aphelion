# Uranus (Planet)

The seventh planet from the Sun — the first planet discovered with a telescope, and the most extreme in the Solar System for its sideways tilt. An ice giant of cyan methane clouds, orbiting in permanent half-century seasons. Mythological counterpart: [[myth-ouranos]]. Crossing note: [[crossing-uranus-ouranos]].

## Key Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Orbital period | 84 Earth years |

## The Sideways Planet

Uranus's axial tilt of 97.77° means it orbits essentially on its side — the poles point roughly toward (and away from) the Sun. The consequence: each pole receives **42 years of continuous sunlight**, then **42 years of continuous darkness**. When Voyager 2 visited in 1986, the south pole was pointed toward the Sun.

Despite this extreme tilt, Uranus's atmosphere is paradoxically the coldest in the Solar System — it radiates almost no internal heat, unlike Jupiter or Saturn. The reason for this thermal suppression is unknown and is one of the planet's major mysteries.

## Discovery and Naming

William Herschel observed Uranus on 13 March 1781 with a 6.2-inch telescope in Bath, England. He initially thought it was a comet. Confirmation as a planet followed. Herschel named it **Georgium Sidus** ("George's Star") in honour of King George III — a politically motivated choice that the rest of Europe refused.

French astronomers called it "Herschel." The name **Uranus** — proposed by Johann Bode, following the convention of naming planets after Roman/Greek deities — was adopted slowly and didn't fully replace rivals until around 1850.

The logic of the name: if Jupiter/Zeus overthrew Saturn/Cronus, who overthrew Uranus/Ouranos, then the planetary sequence outward from Jupiter (Saturn, Uranus) mirrors the mythological succession backwards in time.

## Moons — Shakespeare and Pope

Uranus's 29 moons are uniquely named from characters in **Shakespeare** and **Alexander Pope** — the only major planet system not drawing on Greco-Roman mythology. The five major moons:
- **Miranda** — character in *The Tempest*; has fault canyons 20 km deep
- **Ariel** — *The Tempest* and *The Rape of the Lock*; youngest surface
- **Umbriel** — *The Rape of the Lock*; oldest surface
- **Titania** — Queen of the Fairies in *A Midsummer Night's Dream*; largest moon
- **Oberon** — King of the Fairies in *A Midsummer Night's Dream*

## The Rings

Uranus has 13 ring systems — discovered serendipitously on 10 March 1977 when astronomers were observing a stellar occultation to study the atmosphere and noticed the star dimming briefly before and after disappearing behind Uranus. The rings are made of **extremely dark particles** reflecting only 2% of light — darker than coal. The outermost ring is blue, inner rings grey.
