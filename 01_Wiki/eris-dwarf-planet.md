# Eris (Dwarf Planet)

The most massive known dwarf planet — slightly more massive than Pluto — and a scattered disc object whose 2005 discovery directly triggered the 2006 IAU redefinition of "planet" that demoted Pluto. Named with deliberate mythological irony. A prime example of the `deliberate` crossing type in the [[iau-naming-conventions]] system.

## Key Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Diameter | ~2,326 km (similar to Pluto) |
| Mass | ~27% more massive than Pluto |
| Orbital period | 561 years |
| Perihelion | 38.4 AU |
| Aphelion | 97.7 AU |
| Surface | Bright white methane ice (continuously refreshing) |
| Moon | Dysnomia |

## The Naming — Deliberate Irony

Originally nicknamed "Xena" by discoverer Mike Brown's team (after the TV warrior princess). When the IAU formalised the object, Brown named it **Eris** — the Greek goddess of strife and discord — for a specific, stated reason:

*"Eris caused strife and discord by causing quarrels among people, and that's what this one has done too."*

The discovery of Eris, initially described as a "tenth planet" because it appeared larger than Pluto, forced the IAU to finally define "planet" precisely. The 2006 IAU vote created the dwarf planet category — simultaneously demoting Pluto and accommodating Eris. The goddess of strife had struck the solar system's ordering.

The moon was named **Dysnomia** — Greek goddess of lawlessness, and Eris's daughter. Brown notes this also retained an oblique reference to "Xena" (played on TV by Lucy *Lawless*).

## Scattered Disc Object

Unlike [[sedna-dwarf-planet]], Eris was scattered outward from the [[kuiper-belt]] by gravitational interactions with Neptune. Its highly inclined orbit (44°) is unusual but consistent with scattered disc origin. At aphelion (~97.7 AU), Eris is nearly three times farther from the Sun than Pluto.

## Surface Chemistry

Eris's surface is bright white — one of the highest albedos of any solar system body. Methane ice covers the surface, but spectroscopic observations (JWST) show **no ethane** — unlike [[sedna-dwarf-planet]]. This suggests Eris's surface is **continuously refreshing**: nitrogen-driven convection or methane sublimation and redeposition keeps the surface fresh. The extreme cold (30–56 K) allows this cycle to operate even at great distances from the Sun.

## Tidally Locked System

Eris is tidally locked to its moon Dysnomia (rotation period = Dysnomia's orbital period = 15.78 days). Dysnomia is also locked to Eris — making this the second known double-synchronous system after Pluto-Charon.
