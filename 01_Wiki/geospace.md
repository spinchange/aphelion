# Geospace

The region of [[outer-space]] encompassing Earth's upper atmosphere and magnetosphere. Inner boundary: the ionosphere. Outer boundary: the magnetopause — the interface between Earth's magnetosphere and the [[interplanetary-medium|solar wind]].

## Structure

- **Near-Earth space:** Low Earth orbits (LEO) out to geostationary orbit — the zone of most human space activity; also the zone of highest [[space-debris]] concentration
- **Van Allen radiation belts:** Lie within geospace; trap charged particles from the solar wind
- **Magnetotail:** Solar wind stretches the magnetosphere on the night side to 100–200+ Earth radii
- The lunar surface is shielded from solar wind for ~4 days/month as the Moon passes through the magnetotail

## Space Weather

Geospace conditions are driven by the Sun and solar wind (the domain of [[heliophysics]]). The day-side magnetopause is typically ~10 Earth radii from Earth's centre; compressed by solar-wind pressure.

**Geomagnetic storms** can:
- Increase energetic electron fluxes that permanently damage satellite electronics
- Disrupt shortwave radio and GPS
- Pose radiation hazards to astronauts in LEO
- Create auroras at high latitudes around geomagnetic poles

Electrically charged particles at low density populate geospace; their motion is controlled by Earth's magnetic field and can drive electrical currents into the upper atmosphere.
